Mindanao River Basin TDS simulator

Routed land-cover + population model of total dissolved solids (Montero & Navaluna, University of Southern Mindanao). Move the sliders: every river reach is recomputed in your browser.

Validation on 22 published sites: no structure predicts held-out rivers better than the average of the other sites (best 54.1 vs 52.5 mg/L), and repeat surveys of the same spot differ by 44 mg/L. Treat maps as scenarios, not measurements. Squares are published sites: click one to compare.

Map layers

Use the layer box at the top right of the map to switch on land cover, tree cover, population, elevation, slope, rock type, the Mt Apo volcanic zone and the 30 proposed sampling sites.

Data

Terrain ALOS AW3D30 (OpenTopography); land cover ESA WorldCover 2021; population WorldPop 2020; 22 published TDS surveys. Geology USGS OFR 97-470-F. Imagery © Esri; streets © OpenStreetMap contributors. Built-up doubling is applied to routed totals, an approximation.
